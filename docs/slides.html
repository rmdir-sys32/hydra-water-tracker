01. Problem: Manual Logging Takes Too Many Steps

SLIDE 03 / 10

Typical hydration apps need several screens to record one drink. Each extra step is a reason to skip the log.

UnlockHandset
→
OpenFind and launch app
→
DismissPrompts or ads
→
SelectDrink type and volume
→
SaveConfirm entry

Repetitive

The same flow repeats for every drink, all day.

Form-based

Manual entry adds effort at every intake.

Goal

Reduce each log to one physical action.
